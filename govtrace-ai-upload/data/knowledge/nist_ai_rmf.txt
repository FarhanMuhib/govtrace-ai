NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)

The NIST AI Risk Management Framework helps organizations
manage risks associated with artificial intelligence systems.

The framework contains four core functions:

GOVERN

GOVERN establishes policies, processes, accountability,
organizational culture, roles and responsibilities for
artificial intelligence risk management.

MAP

MAP identifies the context of an AI system, including its
purpose, users, affected groups, potential impacts,
dependencies, and possible risks.

MEASURE

MEASURE involves assessing and monitoring AI risks using
quantitative and qualitative methods. Organizations evaluate
performance, reliability, safety, security, privacy and
fairness.

MANAGE

MANAGE focuses on prioritizing risks and implementing
mitigation strategies, monitoring activities and response
actions.

NIST AI RMF promotes trustworthy AI characteristics,
including validity and reliability, safety, security and
resilience, accountability and transparency, explainability,
privacy enhancement, and fairness.
